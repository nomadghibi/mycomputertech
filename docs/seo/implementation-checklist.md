# SEO Implementation Checklist

## High Impact First

- Align primary city and NAP across website, schema, footer, contact page, and Google Business Profile
- Publish the 10 service landing pages
- Publish the 9 location pages
- Replace homepage copy, title, description, and H1 with the optimized version
- Add internal links from homepage, services, service areas, and blog posts to the new pages
- Implement LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList schema

## Technical SEO

- Confirm final `SITE_URL` / `VITE_SITE_URL` matches production domain
- Regenerate sitemap and robots after page rollout
- Submit updated sitemap in Google Search Console
- Ensure every published page has a canonical URL
- Ensure all indexable pages return `index,follow`
- Add alt text to all newly added images

## Yoast Setup

- Set one focus keyphrase per page
- Ensure title length is under 60 characters
- Ensure meta description is under 155 characters
- Use the focus keyphrase in the intro, at least one H2, image alt text where relevant, and CTA copy naturally
- Keep paragraphs short and readable

## Content Quality

- Use local city mentions naturally, not repetitively
- Do not copy the same intro paragraph across city pages
- Mention actual services only
- Add FAQs that match real customer questions
- Add one strong CTA above the fold and one near the end of each page

## Internal Linking

- Link every service page to at least 2 related service pages
- Link every location page to at least 2 service pages and the contact page
- Link blog posts to relevant money pages
- Link the homepage to priority service and location pages

## Conversion

- Keep phone CTA visible on mobile
- Keep the contact form working and tested after every deployment
- Track form submits and phone clicks in analytics
- Add trust elements near forms and CTAs
